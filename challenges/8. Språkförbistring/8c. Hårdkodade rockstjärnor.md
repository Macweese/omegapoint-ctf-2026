#### Uppdrag

### Hårdkodade rockstjärnor

Till slut lyckas du tyda affischerna: det är AW i dag, med start klockan 13. Du
tittar på klockan. Den är redan halv tre. Inte undra på att ingen verkar jobba
med att lösa incidenten.

Du skyndar dig till AW-rummet. Redan i korridoren hör du musiken: gitarrer,
trummor och en sångare som tar i så mycket att det nästan låter sjukt.

Du känner igen bandet. Det är backend-teamet, riktiga ”stjärnor” som vanligtvis
skriver allt i Rust och vägrar prata om något annat. I dag har de bytt
tangentborden mot elgitarrer och står på ett hopsnickrat scenbygge av gamla
serverlådor.

Du lyssnar noga på vad de sjunger om och inser att texten inte handlar om kärlek
eller om att leva snabbt och dö ung. Den låter mindre som en låt och mer som ett
program.

### Solution

First I transcribed the lyrics:

```
My voice holds forever heartache
The radio is midnight revolution
The doorway is desperation, resurrection, thunderstruck
Rock my voice with the radio
Rock my voice with the doorway
The rebel is unbroken electricity, love
The hour is devastation, imagination, silhouettes
Rock my voice with the rebel
Rock my voice with the ocean
The skyline is beautiful moonlight
The lightning is heartbroken, loneliness, thunder
Rock my voice with the skyline
Rock my voice with the lightning
The silence is we were born to burn
Let my voice be my voice with the silence
Let my voice be my voice without the silence
Young rhythm holds beautiful ashes
The bass is heartache, midnight
The ember is electricity, revolution, forgiveness
Rock your rhythm with the basic
Rock your rhythm with the ember
The flame is beautiful thunder
The heartbeat is imagination, silhouettes
Unbroken, the shadows are devastation, forgiveness, ashes
Rock your rhythm with the flame
Rock your rhythm with the heartbeat
Rock your rhythm with the shadows
A static's turn will music down
Let your rhythm be your rhythm with the static
Let your rhythm be your rhythm without the static
The finale holds beautiful ashes
The storm is imagination's heartbroken thunder
The closing is devastation, heartbroken ashes
Rock our finale with the rebel
Rock our finale with the storm
Rock our finale with the shadows
Rock our finale with the heartbeat
Rock our finale with the closing
Let my voice be my voice with your rhythm
Let my voice be my voice with our finale
Whisper my voice
```

The "Rust" is actually Rockstar, an esoteric language where numbers are written
as words. Each word's length gives one digit, and `Rock X with Y` pushes Y onto
the array X. `Whisper` prints the array as ASCII.

| Variable                                                | Words                      | Decodes to               |
|---------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------|--------------------------|
| `my voice`                                              | "forever heartache" (7, 9) | 79 `O`                   |
| `radio`, `doorway`                                      | 80, 113+… (8,0 / 1,2,3)    | 80 `P`, 123 `{`          |
| `hour` (pushed as "ocean"), `skyline`, `lightning`      |                            | 111 `o`, 99 `c`, 107 `k` |
| `rhythm`, `bass`, `ember`, `flame`, `shadows` (3 words) |                            | `_`, `b`, `e`, `a`, `s`  |
| `finale`, `storm` ("imagination's" counts as 11)        |                            | `_`, `u`                 |

The `with the silence` / `without the silence` and `static` lines cancel out and
don't change the output.

The transcript doesn't decode exactly as written, so I corrected a few lines by
hand:

`rebel`: as written, "unbroken electricity, love" gives 8,1,4 = 814. It
needs 1,1,4 = 114 (`r`), so the first word is probably wrong.

`heartbeat`: "imagination, silhouettes" gives only 11. It needs 116 (`t`),
so a 6-letter word is probably missing.

`closing`: "devastation, heartbroken ashes" gives 115 (`s`). It needs
125 (`}`), so the middle word should have 12 letters, like "resurrection".

Names: "ocean" and "basic" look like mishearings of `hour` and `bass`.

With those fixes the output is `OP{rock` + `_beats` + `_rust}`. I did not run
the program as written, because I had no Rockstar interpreter, but the flag was
accepted.

Flag: `OP{rock_beats_rust}`
